This file shows all the obesity rate with income and market afforadability in different countries.

Column name: variable type: description
  Country: chr(text) : country names
  Obesity Rate: num(continuous): obesity rate in percentage
  Male Obesity Rate: num(countinous): male obesity rate in percentage
  Female Obesity Rate: num(countinous): female obesity rate in percentage
  average_price: num(countinous): average price for each items in item_name column
  item_name: chr(text): name of the market/restaurant foods or goods
  Median Income 2020: num(countinous): median income in dollar
  Mean Income 2020: num(countinous): mean income in dollar
  GDP per Capita 2020: num(countinous): GDP per capita
